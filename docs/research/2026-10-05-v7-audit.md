# V7: profit, data integrity, and preserved results

Evidence snapshot: October 5, 2026, 19:56 PDT / October 6, 02:56 UTC.
This is a dated paper-trading audit, not a promise of future returns.

The measured problem is a weak net edge combined with poor execution capture.
Research V6 earned $17.93 after its winners earned $504.26 and losers cost
$486.34. More trades alone have not solved this: the archived motion experiment
resolved 512 positions and lost $0.08. V7 fixes reproducible correctness defects,
preserves the losing evidence, and establishes a separate evaluation era.

## What prevented better results

1. **Loss magnitude almost erased a high win rate.** V6 had 184 winning and 71
   losing logical positions: 72.16% wins, with an average $2.74 win and $6.85
   loss. At those observed payouts, break-even is about 71.42% wins. That tiny
   margin explains why a high hit rate produced little net profit. Atlanta
   contributed −$123.98; its September 19 and September 11 logical positions
   lost $46.26 and $45.27. These are measured loss concentrations, not proof
   that banning Atlanta will improve future returns.
2. **Most requested size never filled.** V6 filled 5,312.96 of 108,965 requested
   contracts, 4.88%. There were 773 expired roots out of 1,033 valid roots.
   Requested size is not executable capacity, and this ratio is not a calibrated
   passive-order fill probability. Main already contains bounded two-level
   taker crossing, liquidity-aware scan ordering, and research quote-lifetime
   improvements that were absent from the verified production release.
3. **The production release lagged the fixes.** September's partial-loss memory
   and conservative sizing corrections were present in main but absent from
   the verified host source. The preserved September 13 journal review in
   [the incident audit](../audits/2026-09-12-strategy-performance.md) documented
   six incorrectly vetoed stop attempts after a partial exit. This is retained
   dated evidence; the underlying monitor journal was not queried afresh here,
   and no counterfactual dollar savings are claimed.
4. **Historical daily maxima could be formed from incomplete inputs.** The
   forecaster accepted a partial day's hourly values as a daily maximum and
   the truth loader allowed overlapping forecast sources to replace one another
   without an explicit source policy. Reproductions demonstrate invalid model
   inputs and unreliable evaluation provenance. Their historical dollar impact
   cannot be identified from the available journal alone.
5. **Paper exit evidence overstated what one book could support.** Multiple
   positions in one account could reuse the same displayed bid depth during a
   monitor pass. Recorded quote time could also reflect execution time instead
   of fetch time. V7 conserves depth within each separate account and records
   fetch-time evidence, rejecting stale quotes. This can reduce reported paper
   performance; it improves realism, not inherently profitability. Current-main's
   unique active-position index ordinarily prevents duplicate same-side exposure;
   shared-depth conservation is a defensive correction for legacy duplicates,
   not a demonstrated cause of the production profit shortfall.
6. **Separate paper accounts interfered with each other's maker fills.** Finite
   trade tape was pooled across economic accounts despite their independent
   ledgers. V7 conserves finite volume across orders within each account,
   independently simulates each account, and retains historical closed-order
   claims across restarts. Legacy per-order shadows retain their documented
   semantics; orphaned claims conservatively reduce available volume. This is
   an execution-simulation defect, not a quantified profit opportunity.
7. **Calibration diagnostics used the realized answer to select uncertainty.**
   Two retrospective diagnostic paths selected a temperature cohort from final
   truth, then used that cohort's sigma to score the prediction. V7 chooses
   cohorts from the prediction before truth, reports postprocessors' own-sigma
   Brier scores separately, and labels in-sample, shared-sigma and stitched
   diagnostics. Those diagnostics cannot qualify a model for promotion.
8. **Durability and display omitted useful history.** Nightly archives omitted
   maker allocations, volume claims, frozen goals, and several settlement and
   research records. The dashboard did not expose every archived profile.
   V7 adds these records and depth partitions to the archive and renders all
   archived profiles, including zero-result V2, losing V5, V6, motion, and legacy
   history. No earlier loss or unsettled position is rewritten as a V7 result.
   New append-only served-forecast evidence records named members, retrieval
   and recording clocks, station climate windows, fit lead, parameters, training
   digest and serving controls. UPDATE, DELETE and REPLACE are rejected.
   Unexposed provider initialization and hourly completeness remain NULL;
   current-run inputs are not backdated into historical forecast vintages.
9. **Deployment capacity is blocked.** The journal occupied 30.98 GiB, with
   20.23 GiB free. The canonical backup/restore verification needs one snapshot
   plus 1 GiB, approximately 31.98 GiB free. Production remains operational;
   source installation was deferred rather than bypassing the preservation
   gate. Storage remediation and verified cutover remain required.
10. **Evidence is stale or missing where it matters.** Full historical NO-side
   book depth, maker queue position, decision-time forecast initialization,
   and paired current model scores are not established for all old cases.
   Phoenix had three unresolved final-truth verification warnings for September
   27 in the public artifact. V7 cannot backfill those gaps with invented truth,
   fills, or later forecasts.
11. **Some backtests learned truth unavailable at serving.** EMOS, analog and
    cohort diagnostics used observations before the target but after its nominal
    serve date. Lead-aware cutoffs now restrict truth to before target minus lead.
    The matched challenger now follows production's 45-calendar-day, deadband,
    bias-only correction; the old 60-row dispersion adjustment was a different
    model. Duplicates count once and conflicting cases invalidate diagnostics.
12. **Forecast accuracy bypassed dataset promotion and sampling boundaries.**
    Dataset guidance accepted an accuracy-candidate label without explicit
    after-cost live approval, could mix another station's features, and could
    select inputs unavailable at the target. Repeated vintages inflated MOS
    sample counts. V7 requires approved keys, after-cost approval, KSFO scope,
    positive lead and observed availability; MOS counts eligible station-days.
    New research reports explicitly block automatic promotion.
13. **A forecast report could use the wrong distribution.** A stale independent
    EMOS lookup could replace the snapshot's own mean/spread. V7 validates their
    coupling before intraday adjustment, consistent with the scan path.
14. **Legacy negative settlement labels were parsed as positive.** Signed and
    decimal thresholds/ranges now retain their signs and explicit separators.
    Existing outcomes are not automatically rewritten; contract-truth evidence
    and an explicit restatement are required to change old financial results.
15. **Publication retries were incomplete.** The previously separate retry repair
    is integrated in V7 and supplies bounded additional retries and URL fallbacks for transient Pages
    failures. This addresses publication reliability, not weather-model edge.
16. **Static deployment could publish a mixed or unverified app.** Follow-up
    review reproduced a partial transfer replacing the live index while deleting
    an old asset. V7 now stages and hashes the complete app, excludes runtime
    JSON, uses atomic exchange under the publisher's exact lock, and verifies
    the exact successful Pages deployment plus public static/runtime hashes.
    Failure restores and republishes the previous app. This fixes delivery
    correctness; it does not change the trading model.
17. **Settlement recovery advice overstated what auto-settle does.** A fresh
    October 5, 21:20 PDT source check found the September 27 Phoenix
    [post-day CLI](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/nwstext/202609280832-KPSR-CDUS45-CLIPHX)
    reports its observed maximum as missing (`MM`); IEM also records a missing
    high. None of 22 inspected CLI products supplies a qualifying numeric
    post-day correction. The later 100°F report belongs to September 26.
    [Monthly CF6](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/nwstext/202610032207-KPSR-CXUS55-CF6PHX)
    lists 97°F for September 27, but its final-posting marker does not establish
    final data under [NWS Instruction 10-1004, section 4.3.2](https://www.weather.gov/media/directives/010_pdfs/pd01010004curr.pdf).
    V7 corrects the alert's false claim that auto-settle fetches older CLISFO.
    Recovery requires verified final truth for the affected station/date and
    subsequent verification; all three positions remain unresolved. Neither a
    missing value nor a different product is silently converted into a payout.
    The physical reason for missing temperatures and monetary impact remain
    unknown. Raw source files and hashes are retained in private operator evidence.
18. **Installed build dependencies could lag the locked source.** During this
    release, the primary checkout's ignored `node_modules` still contained
    MapLibre 5.24.0 despite source and lockfile pinning 6.4.1. The first complete
    static build ran with that old installed dependency tree. A frozen install then
    restored 6.4.1, all 211 web tests passed, and the app was rebuilt and
    replaced through the guarded publisher. The corrected public build and
    runtime hashes were verified at Pages commit `802333808a052868e57a14be2874820f702f32b8`.
    Future deployment now requires a successful frozen install before build or
    transfer. This was a deployment-process error, not measured trading loss;
    no paper journal, backend policy, or billing setting changed.

Closed V6 positions contributed −$324.69; settled positions contributed +$342.61.
This does **not** show that removing stops would recover $324.69: exits selected
troubled positions while surviving positions were allowed to settle. Likewise,
settled-only calibration is biased by survival and exit selection. Score the
forecast against final station truth for every eligible city/day, including
skips and early exits, before drawing a calibration conclusion.

## Research program through October 31

Five percent of a $1,000 initial account is $50/day; $40/day is 4%. At V6's
observed 0.456% return on resolved entry capital, $40 would arithmetically require
about $8,765 of resolved entry turnover per day. This is a scale diagnostic,
not an estimate of executable liquidity or a future profit forecast. Raising
capital cannot create edge or depth. The current data does not support a
5%-daily expectation or a $40/day deadline commitment.

The quant and ML work should prioritize measured edge, uncertainty, and
execution over a more complex predictor:

- **October 6–9: operational baseline.** Resolve backup capacity, complete a
  verified V7 cutover, reconcile exactly two active separate ledgers, refresh
  analysis, verify publication hashes and all timer definitions, and preserve
  old exposure. Keep real-money execution disabled. Record fetch times, model
  identifiers, fixed-standard station-day windows, full hourly coverage,
  source and initialization provenance, book depth, quote amendments, expiry,
  adverse selection, fees, and every veto. Unavailable provenance stays unknown.
- **October 10–16: clean forecast comparison.** Rebuild source-separated,
  complete-day datasets; retain the existing champion as a benchmark. Compare
  regularized EMOS and a small ensemble residual challenger on identical
  forward city/day folds. Optimize proper distribution scores such as CRPS;
  also report Brier/log scores for settlement brackets, interval coverage,
  bias, and market-relative skill by station, lead, season, and spread. Pool
  sparse cities with shrinkage rather than tuning each city's thresholds on
  its largest past loss. Fit transforms on training folds only.
- **October 17–24: executable edge experiments.** Predeclare a small fixed family
  of research candidates before evaluation. Compare maker reservation prices
  and TTLs, bounded depth-aware taker entries, and forecast-based versus
  liquidity-based exits using retained decision-time data. Estimate fill
  probability conditional on price, queue and time to close; report net edge
  conditional on fills and adverse selection. Do not infer fills from later
  candle highs or share real depth across economically separate accounts.
  Use paired dates, embargo overlapping weather targets, and retain untraded
  opportunities and zero days. Incomplete old evidence remains exploratory.
- **October 25–31: prospective decision.** Freeze the candidate and its
  fingerprint, then report every calendar day's after-fee P&L, fixed-initial
  and day-start ROI, drawdown, loss concentration, filled volume, opportunity
  capture, forecast scores, and publication freshness. Report $40 and $50
  attainment separately. Promotion requires the repository's existing readiness
  and paired model-evidence gates; a larger account or looser entry gate needs
  new validated capacity/edge evidence. The October period is a feasibility
  checkpoint and may be too short for qualification.

This is a proposed research schedule, not an installed automation or a claim
that those experiments have already passed. The existing immutable experiment
and paired evidence tables provide one canonical record for predeclared
candidates; avoid creating parallel ad hoc spreadsheets as a second authority.

## Primary methods and platform references

- [Gneiting and Raftery: proper scoring rules](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf)
  supports distribution scoring and comparison on the same forecast cases.
- [Open-Meteo Single Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api) exposes
  individual initialization times. [Previous Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api)
  is a different historical product; a stitched hourly lead series must not be
  described as a known forecast available at an earlier decision time.
- [Official prediction-market order-book documentation](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/orderbook_responses)
  describes bid-only books, complementary asks, fractional sizes, and dollar
  string fields. Capacity must come from observed executable depth, with
  current market-specific fees, rather than a requested order quantity.
- [Busseti, Ryu and Boyd: risk-constrained Kelly](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/kelly.html)
  provides a research framework for trading growth against drawdown risk.
  V7 retains the existing lower-bound quarter-Kelly controls; it does not claim
  to implement that paper's optimizer or guarantee its theoretical bound.

## Reproduction and release validation

Use a fresh read-only operator export, not the MacBook's ignored database:

```bash
python scripts/audit_paper_performance.py \
  --export /path/to/fresh-runtime-export.json \
  --strategy /path/to/matching-strategy-research.json \
  --output /path/to/baseline.json
```

The export includes `captured_at`, `paper.paper_accounts.rows` and
`paper.paper_orders.rows`. The command requires an explicit input, rejects
future bookings and invalid capital/targets, keeps separate accounts and
unassigned histories, includes losing partial exits, and never synthesizes
ledger equity from P&L.

The forecast inventory and reconstructed score diagnostic use the separately
captured bounded export:

```bash
python scripts/audit_forecast_evidence.py \
  --export /path/to/fresh-weather-export.json \
  --output /path/to/forecast-baseline.json
```

This command requires complete table exports, final station truth and elapsed
climate days. It rejects conflicting cases and future retrieval clocks, reports
excluded sources and uses the existing Gaussian CRPS primitive. It does not
retrain a model or establish original forecast vintages.

The pilot reuses the same score implementation and the production correction
helpers, without searching parameters:

```bash
python scripts/evaluate_v7_bias_pilot.py --repo . \
  --export /path/to/fresh-weather-export.json \
  --output /path/to/bias-pilot.json
```

It also writes a deterministic `.lineage.jsonl.gz` containing every paired
prediction. The primary result requires a complete 45-day exported training
window; all-date and left-edge comparisons remain labeled supplemental.
